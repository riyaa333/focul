How it works

Take a look inside Focul.

A 15-minute focus loop that captures itself, organises itself, and hands your work forward to the next session.

01 · FOCUS

Start the timer.
Go heads-down.

Fifteen minutes on the clock, living in your menu bar. No notifications, no noise, just the work in front of you until the bell.

02 · BELL

The bell rings.
Time to debrief.

When the session ends, Focul gently prompts you. Hold one key and the recording starts. No app to open, no doc to find.

03 · SPEAK

Talk for 60 seconds.
However it comes out.

Ramble, think out loud, contradict yourself. Focul transcribes the whole thing, the "um"s and tangents included, and finds the signal.

04 · BRIEFING

Tasks appear.
The loop closes.

Sixty seconds of talking becomes a clean, tagged list of actions, blockers, and calendar holds, saved to today and waiting when you start your next session.
