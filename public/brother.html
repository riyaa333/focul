how to install

  1. Open the .dmg you just downloaded.
  2. Drag Focul into your Applications folder.
  3. Eject the disk image (right-click the Focul drive on your desktop → Eject).
  4. Open Terminal (Spotlight → "Terminal") and paste this, then hit return:
    xattr -cr /Applications/Focul.app
    This strips the quarantine flag macOS slaps on unsigned downloads. Without it you'll get a fake "is damaged" warning.
  5. Open Focul from your Applications folder. Done.
