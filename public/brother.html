how to install — follow exactly

  1. Open the Focul.dmg you just downloaded. A window pops up with the Focul icon.
  2. Drag the Focul icon into your Applications folder. Do NOT double-click Focul inside the disk image window — that's the trap that shows a fake "is damaged" warning.
  3. Eject the disk image: find Focul in Finder's left sidebar (under Locations) → click the ⏏ eject icon next to it. Or right-click the Focul drive on your desktop → Eject.
  4. Open Terminal (press ⌘ Space, type "Terminal", hit return). Paste this exact line, then hit return:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Focul.app
    Nothing will print. That means it worked. This strips the quarantine flag macOS adds to every download.
  5. Open Finder → Applications → double-click Focul. It should launch normally.
Still seeing "Focul is damaged"? You're opening it from the disk image, not Applications. Click Eject Disk Image on the warning, then open Focul from /Applications.
